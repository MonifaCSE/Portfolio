Overview

BuildHub is a comprehensive single-vendor e-commerce platform designed to manage sales of heavy machinery, power tools, hand tools, building materials, electrical, and plumbing products. It features role-based administration, complete catalog management, and automated stock handling.

Key System Capabilities

01

Product & Inventory Management

CRUD operations for products, categories, brands, campaigns, and stock levels.

02

Role-Based Administration

Granular administrative permissions for products, order processing, and promotions.

03

Promotions & Discounts Engine

Support for promotional campaigns, category discounts, and checkout coupons.

04

Order Processing & Returns

Complete workflow handling for customer orders, wishlist management, and product returns.

Overview

BuildHub was created to solve inventory and order management challenges for building material suppliers. The system structures single-vendor operations into clean database entities with role-based back-office controls.

Key Features

  • Product Catalog: Multi-category hierarchy supporting tools, machinery, and electrical supplies.
  • Inventory Tracking: Real-time stock status, SKU tracking, and threshold alerts.
  • Role-Based Admin Panel: Managed views for warehouse operations, promotion management, and sales reporting.
